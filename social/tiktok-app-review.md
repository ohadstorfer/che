# TikTok app review text

TikTok app `Posta Spanish` (7703575300685236244). The form can't be saved without a demo video, so its text is kept here.

Posta is a language-learning app for Argentine Spanish (postaspanish.com). This integration is the Posta team's own publishing tool: it posts Posta's original lessons and short videos to the official Posta TikTok account. It is used by our team only and is not offered to other creators.

Login Kit (user.info.basic): the account owner signs in with TikTok to connect the Posta account. We read the open ID, display name and avatar to show which account will post.

Content Posting API (video.publish): after the account owner reviews a post, chooses its privacy and interaction settings and confirms, the tool publishes the video or photo post directly to the connected account.

Content Posting API (video.upload): sends a video to the account's TikTok inbox as a draft, so the owner can finish it in TikTok when it needs editing.

All media is created by Posta and served from our verified domain.
