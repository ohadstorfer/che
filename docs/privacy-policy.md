Posta is an app for learning Argentine Spanish. It is run by PICAWAVE, LLC, a Delaware limited liability company ("we", "us"), which is responsible for your data. This policy explains what we collect, why, who helps us run the app, how long we keep it, and how to delete it.

Questions: contact@picawave.com

## The short version

- We collect what Posta needs to work: your account, your progress, and, if you use Hablar, your voice and your conversations with Pancho.
- We use analytics, including recordings of how you use the app, and crash reports, to fix problems and improve Posta.
- We don't show ads, we don't sell your data, and we don't track you across other apps or websites.
- Voice recordings are deleted after 30 days. You can delete your account and your data at any time from inside the app.

## What we collect

**Your account**
- When you first use Posta, we create a guest account for you. It has a random ID and no name or email.
- If you save your account with Apple or Google, we receive your email address and, if you share it, your name. With Apple you can choose to hide your real email; we then get a private relay address from Apple. We never see a password.
- Your time zone, so your streak and reminders follow your day.
- Your answers to the first-time questions: the name you want to be called, why you are learning, your level, how much time you have each day, and what made learning hard before. We use them to build your plan.

**Your learning**
- Your progress: lessons finished, the answers you give, words you've practiced, mistakes, and your streak.
- Answers you report to us as wrongly marked.
- When you tap "Why?" under a wrong answer, your answer and the exercise are sent to our AI provider to write the explanation.

**Hablar (speaking practice)**
- When you talk with Pancho, we record your voice, turn it into text, and keep the transcript of the conversation, what you typed instead of speaking, the hints and translations you asked for, and the feedback you got.
- Posta uses the microphone only while you are recording, and only if you allow it.
- Pancho's replies, the feedback and the explanations are generated by AI, and Pancho's voice is computer-generated.
- **Voice recordings are deleted automatically after 30 days.** Transcripts stay with your account until you delete it.
- Please don't say or type sensitive personal information (such as health details or ID numbers) in Hablar.

**Purchases**
- If you subscribe, the App Store or Google Play handles the payment. We never see your card details.
- We receive from Apple or Google whether your subscription is active, its plan, its renewal or end date, and any refund. Each purchase is tagged with your Posta user ID (not your email) so we can match it to your account.
- We record the steps you take on the subscription screen (for example, that you opened it or started a purchase), and the promo codes and offers you use.

**Notifications**
- If you allow notifications, we store a push token for your device and the reminder time you chose, so we can send practice reminders. You can turn them off at any time in the app or in your device settings.
- Reminders before a free trial or free week ends are scheduled on your phone and don't send anything to us.

**Feedback**
- If you send us feedback from inside the app, we keep your messages, any screenshots or photos you attach, which screen you were on, the app version, and your phone model and system version. The app takes a screenshot of the screen you were on, which you can remove before sending. Photos are read from your library only if you pick them.

**Analytics and session recordings**
- We use PostHog to understand how Posta is used: which screens you open, what you tap, and how long things take.
- PostHog also makes **session recordings**: a replay of what appeared on your screen while you used the app. The recordings are not masked, so they show what you read and what you type in the app (for example, your answers). They also include the app's technical logs and the timing of its requests to our servers. We use them to see where people get stuck and to fix bugs.
- This data is tied to your Posta user ID, not to your name or email. PostHog uses your IP address to estimate your approximate location (country and city) and then discards it: the IP address itself is not stored with your data.

**Crash reports**
- If the app crashes or hits an error, Sentry sends us a report with what went wrong, what the app was doing just before, your phone model, system version and app version, and your Posta user ID.

**Emails**
- If you saved your account with Apple or Google and haven't subscribed, we may send you a few emails in the weeks after, about your plan, Hablar and Posta Premium, including a discount offer. Every email has an unsubscribe link, and one tap stops them.
- We also write to you if you contact us, or about your account when we need to.

**Technical data**
- Like any online service, our servers and providers see your IP address and basic device information (such as phone model and system version) when the app connects to them, including when it downloads lesson audio or checks for app updates. We use it to keep Posta running and secure.

**On your device only**
- Downloaded lessons, some settings, and what the home-screen widget shows (your streak and the word of the day) stay on your phone.

We don't collect your precise location, your contacts or your advertising ID. We do not use advertising, we do not sell or "share" your data for advertising, and we do not track you across other apps or websites.

## Why we use it

- To run your account and save your progress across devices.
- To build your plan, lessons and reviews around what you know.
- To give you speaking practice and feedback in Hablar.
- To check your subscription, unlock Premium and apply offers.
- To send the reminders you asked for, and the emails described above.
- To answer your feedback, fix crashes and bugs, keep Posta secure, and improve the course and the app.

## Who helps us run Posta

We share data only with the services that make the app work, and only what each one needs. They process it on our behalf, under contracts that limit how they can use it.

| Service | What it does | What it receives |
|---|---|---|
| Supabase | Database, sign-in, file storage and our server functions | Your account, progress, transcripts, recordings, purchases and feedback (including screenshots) |
| Anthropic (Claude) | Pancho's replies, feedback, hints, translations and answer explanations | The text of your Hablar conversations and the answers you ask about |
| ElevenLabs | Turns your voice into text, and gives Pancho his voice | Your Hablar voice recordings, and the text Pancho says |
| PostHog | Analytics and session recordings | Screens, taps, session recordings, technical logs, device information, IP address and your Posta user ID |
| Sentry | Crash and error reports | Crash details, device information and your Posta user ID |
| Resend | Sends our emails | Your email address, first name and the text of the email |
| Amazon Web Services | Delivers the lesson audio and hosts the website | Your IP address, when the app downloads audio or you visit the site |
| Expo | Delivers push notifications and app updates | Your device's push token, the text of the reminder, and basic device information |
| Apple / Google | Sign-in, payments and push notifications | That you signed in to Posta, purchase details tagged with your Posta user ID (not your email), and your device's push token |

Anthropic does not use the data we send through its API to train its models. We may also disclose data if the law requires it, to protect people's safety or our rights, or if Posta changes hands, in which case this policy keeps applying to your data.

## Where your data is stored

We are based in the United States, and our providers store and process data in the United States and other countries. If you live in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, we rely on the safeguards the law provides for these transfers, such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses in our providers' data processing terms.

## How we protect it

Data travels between the app and our servers over encrypted connections. Your voice recordings and screenshots are stored privately, and your progress and conversations can only be read from your own account. Only our small team can access the systems that hold your data, and only to run Posta. No system is perfectly secure, so we can't promise absolute security.

## How long we keep it

- Voice recordings, and Pancho's recorded replies: 30 days.
- Reminder delivery logs: 14 days. The record of which reminders we sent you: 120 days.
- Analytics, session recordings and crash reports: deleted by PostHog and Sentry on their own schedule, within 12 months at most.
- Everything else: for as long as you have an account.
- When you delete your account, we delete your data right away. Some copies may remain in our backups for a short time before they are overwritten.
- We keep a record of each store purchase's status (for example, that it was refunded), without your account, so a refunded subscription can't be unlocked again.

## Deleting your account

In the app, tap the capybara picture at the top of the home screen, then **Delete account**. This works for guest accounts too. It permanently deletes your account, progress, Hablar transcripts and recordings, and the feedback and screenshots you sent us. See what gets deleted: https://postaspanish.com/delete-account

Deleting your account does **not** cancel a subscription. Cancel it in your App Store or Google Play settings.

You can also ask us to delete your data, including analytics and crash data, by writing to contact@picawave.com.

## Your rights

Depending on where you live, you can ask us to see the data we hold about you, get a copy of it, correct it, delete it or limit how we use it, and you can object to how we use it. Write to contact@picawave.com and we'll reply within 30 days. We may need to confirm that the request comes from you. We won't treat you differently for using these rights.

**EU, UK and Swiss users.** We use your data because we need it to provide Posta to you under our Terms of Use; because you allowed it (the microphone, notifications and photos), which you can take back at any time in your device settings; and because we have a legitimate interest in keeping Posta secure, fixing and improving it (analytics, session recordings and crash reports), and in telling you about Posta Premium by email, which you can stop at any time. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

**California users.** In the last 12 months we collected the categories described above: identifiers (such as your user ID and email), commercial information (your purchases), internet or app activity (how you use the app), audio, approximate location, and inferences about your level and goals. We collect them for the purposes in "Why we use it" and disclose them only to the service providers listed above. We do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising, and we don't use sensitive personal information to infer things about you. You have the right to know, delete and correct your data, and you can use an authorized agent to do it.

## Children

Posta is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect data from them. If you think a child has given us data, contact us and we'll delete it.

## Our website

The Posta website has no analytics and no advertising cookies. It is hosted by Amazon Web Services and loads its fonts from Google, which receive your IP address when a page opens.

If you join the waitlist on the website, we collect your name and the email address or phone number you give us. We use them only to tell you when Posta is out, and we store them with Supabase. To be taken off the list, write to contact@picawave.com.

## Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. For important changes, we'll let you know in the app before they apply.
