## Hooks that work for us

1. **"Only 1% of Spanish learners understand this."** Play a fast Argentine line, then explain it.
2. **"Spain vs Argentina."** The same thing said two ways (*tú tienes* vs *vos tenés*, *coger*, *pibe*).
3. **"Can you understand Messi?"** A real clip, first at full speed and then slowly, word by word.
4. **"Your Spanish teacher never taught you this."** Vos, *che*, *boludo*, *re*, *posta*.
5. **"I talked to a capybara in Argentine Spanish."** Show a chat with Pancho on screen.
