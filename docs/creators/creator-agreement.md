# Posta Creator Agreement

*A short, plain agreement. It can be accepted by replying "I agree" by email or DM, or signed. This is a template, not legal advice: have a lawyer look at it before larger deals.*

**Between:** Picawave ("Posta", "we") and **{CREATOR NAME}** ({HANDLE}) ("you").
**Starts:** {DATE}

## 1. What you do
You make and post videos about the Posta app on your own channels, in your own style. You mention your personal code **{CODE}**. Unless we agree otherwise in writing (for example "1 video in the first 30 days"), there's no minimum.

## 2. What your followers get
With your code, new subscribers get the first month free on Posta's yearly plan, where the store supports it. We may change the offer with 14 days' notice. Codes already used keep the offer they got.

## 3. What you earn
- **$10 USD** for each new learner who uses your code and makes their first yearly payment.
- **$3 USD** for each new learner who uses your code and makes their second monthly payment.
- One payment per learner, and only for people who weren't already paying for Posta.
- Each commission is held for **14 days**. If the payment is refunded in that time, there's no commission. After 14 days it's yours, even if it's refunded later.
- Fixed fees per video, if any: **{FEE or "none"}**, paid within 14 days of the video going live.

## 4. How and when we pay
At the start of each month we pay everything that's ready, through **{PayPal / Wise}** to **{account}**. Amounts under $20 roll over to the next month. You can see your numbers at any time on your private dashboard. Our records (the app stores' payment data) are what count. If you think something's wrong, tell us within 60 days.

## 5. Fair play
These don't count, and can end the agreement right away:
- Using your own code, or codes from accounts you control.
- Paying or rewarding people to download or subscribe.
- Fake reviews, bots, or misleading claims (for example "free app", "fluent in a month").
- Running paid ads on the word "Posta" or its name in app stores without our OK.

## 6. Ads disclosure
You mark every video as a paid partnership (#ad or the platform's label), as the law requires (for example the FTC in the US or the ASA in the UK).

## 7. Content
The videos are yours. You allow us to repost or share them on Posta's own channels, with credit to you. Using them as paid ads needs your separate OK, and we may pay for that.

## 8. Brand
You can use the Posta name, logo and app screenshots only in videos about Posta. Don't present yourself as part of the Posta team.

## 9. Ending it
Either of us can end this at any time with 7 days' notice by email or DM. Your code then stops working for new people. Commissions earned before the end, including ones still in the 14-day hold, are paid as normal.

## 10. The rest
You're an independent creator, not our employee. You handle your own taxes. If we need a tax form (for example W-9 / W-8BEN), we'll ask. This agreement is the whole deal on this subject, and any change must be in writing (email is fine).
