## Taught words that would have to change (46)

These need a new taught word, not a sentence fix. They go to stage 3.

- **c01#5** Target 'tostada' (singular, otro-cafe and practica-hola groups): in a café porteños order 'tostadas' (plural) or 'un tostado'; the singular only fits at home (¿Otra tostada?). Move the ordering drills to 'tostadas'.
- **c01#13** Targets 'encantado'/'encantada': correct but a bit formal next to vos and che; the everyday porteño form is 'un gusto' (or 'mucho gusto', already taught).
- **c05#8** Target words cuesta / cuestan (cuanto-sale and practica-me-cobras groups: 40fc879b, a082b6be, 5b8fb20f, 38de76a7, d998f606, 0732e38a, ea7b7f23…) are the textbook form; the porteño form is sale / salen (¿Cuánto sale el café?, ¿Cuánto salen las empanadas?). Not patched because the target word must stay.
- **c07#10** entonces: the unit teaches it as the story sequence word (primero, entonces, al final). Porteños order a story with 'después'; 'entonces' is mostly 'so' (consequence). Consider teaching después for sequence and entonces = so.
- **c08#2** busco, buscás, buscame, buscar (pick up): the commoner porteño form is 'pasar a buscar' / 'venir a buscar' (te paso a buscar a las ocho; pasame a buscar por el boliche). Bare 'te busco' is also used, so optional.
- **c08#7** recibí, recibiste with messages/audios/videos (7d836c06, 1c919d4a, 62ed3b7a, c1caf4df, 4f03d852, later ef3eedc4, d437efe6): porteños say 'me llegó tu mensaje' / '¿te llegó el audio?'. Recibir is fine for paquete, plata, mail.

- **c08#30** escuela / escuelas (f2ce2670, e5e3348e, c729a65b and the 'escuelas' group): porteños usually say 'colegio' (¿a qué colegio ibas?). Dropping 'escuelas' as a new word is out of scope here.
- **c12#6** descargá: porteños mostly say bajá / bajate (Bajate la app, es gratis). Consider teaching bajá beside descargá.
- **c12#13** estarás (to someone's face): porteños say 'debés estar…' (Debés estar cansado después del viaje). Keep estarás mostly for questions.
- **c12#14** vendrá in 5c3a4de8 / 2ba7f20d: porteño forms are 'Seguro que Martín no viene, está enfermo' and 'No sé si va a venir'.
- **c12#16** costará: for prices porteños wonder with saldrá (¿Cuánto saldrá?), as the course teaches ¿Cuánto sale?. Teach saldrá instead of or beside costará.

- **c13#27** busques, tomes, reserves, lleves in 'Conviene que…' lines (1445d88f, 7aac68fb, 2ed1cccd, 72c70938, 9f662672, 3cdd2bd8, ca037663, a4b1803a, 74bfd98f, 78137925): the porteño form is 'te conviene + infinitive' (te conviene buscar / tomar / reservar / llevar).
