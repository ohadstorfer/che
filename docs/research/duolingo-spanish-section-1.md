# Duolingo Spanish (from English), section 1 — structure notes

Read on 2026-09-15 from the course path, the section details page and the ten
unit Guidebooks, on a logged-in account. **Structure only, in our own words** —
topics, grammar, counts and order. No sentences or tips are copied; we don't
reuse Duolingo content.

Their course is neutral/Latin-American Spanish with tú, so its vocabulary is
irrelevant to us. What transfers is scope, pacing and sequencing.

## The section

- 8 sections in the course: 1–3 A1, 4 A2, 5–6 B1, 7–8 B2.
- Section 1 is "very early A1": essential phrases and simple grammar. Grammar
  concepts it names: present tense, agreement, formality, adjectives.
- 10 units. Section 2 starts at guidebook id 11.

## Units

Each unit is named for a real-world task. Each Guidebook has exactly 5 key
phrases (short, 3–8 words) and 1–3 grammar tips.

| # | Task | Grammar introduced | Vocabulary domain |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Order at a café | y / o | drinks, snacks; "I want … please" as a chunk |
| 2 | Greet and say goodbye | ser, yo form only (introductions) | greetings, farewells, names |
| 3 | Say where you're from | ser de for origin; adds the él/ella form | countries, "nice to meet you" |
| 4 | Introduce family and friends | noun gender -o/-a; un/una agree with the noun; tener (yo) | family members; possessive mi |
| 5 | Describe personalities | adjective gender agreement; -e/consonant adjectives don't change | personality adjectives; tu; "what is X like?" |
| 6 | Say where your things are | estar for location (está); negation with no before the verb | travel items; aquí |
| 7 | Places in the city | hay (there is/are, singular = plural); en for location | shops, buildings, city |
| 8 | Discuss languages | regular -ar present, yo and tú only; **subject pronouns are optional** | languages, studying, time of day |
| 9 | Talk about the weather | hace + weather noun (no "it"); en + season; yes/no questions by intonation | weather, seasons, clothing |
| 10 | Shop for fruit at a market | plural -s; re-teaches yes/no questions and hay | fruit, numbers 1–3, prices |

## What it teaches us

1. **One idea per unit at the start.** Units 1–5 carry one grammar point each;
   later units two or three. A verb arrives one person at a time: ser as yo
   (unit 2), then él/ella (unit 3); -ar verbs as yo/tú only (unit 8).
2. **Task before grammar.** Unit 1 is a transaction (ordering) held together by
   a fixed chunk and "and/or" — no verb paradigm at all. Greetings and ser come
   second.
3. **Late, explicit pronoun drop.** Early sentences keep the subject pronoun;
   unit 8 says outright that it is optional. (Our accepted answers already allow
   both from unit 1 — we should also *say* it, in a tip, around the first
   regular verbs.)
4. **Negation waits until unit 6.** Plurals wait until unit 10.
5. **The last unit consolidates.** Unit 10 re-teaches hay and yes/no questions
   next to one new point.
6. **Volume grows by review, not by new material.** New-material nodes stay at
   2 per unit while practice, stories and listening grow from 1 node to 8–10.
   Stories appear from unit 4, listening episodes from unit 2.

## Against our outline (docs/course/section-1.yaml, 24 units)

- **Scope.** Their whole section 1 ≈ grammar from our units 1–4, 7 and 9, plus
  pieces we hold until 16 (adjective agreement) and 21 (weather). Our section 1
  runs to things they put much later: gustar, tener que, -er/-ir verbs, the
  vos imperative, reflexives, the present progressive, ir a.
- **Density.** Our units tag 3–7 grammar points each from unit 1 (unit 1: greetings, yo, vos, ser
  1sg and 2sg, llamarse 1sg and 2sg). Theirs tag 1.
- **Shape.** Every unit of ours is 5 lessons of new material. Theirs is
  2 new-material nodes plus a growing share of review, stories and listening.

Questions this raises for us:

- Split our 24 units into ~3 sections (≈ their A1 sections 1–3)?
- Cap grammar at one point per unit for the first units, and introduce
  paradigms a person at a time?
- Open with a transactional unit (ordering at a café/kiosco is very Argentine)
  before ser?
- Plan non-new-material lessons (practice, stories, listening) into each unit's
  count, instead of every lesson teaching new forms?
